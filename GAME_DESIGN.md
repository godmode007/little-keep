# Little Keep — Kids Game Design

A **simple single-player** kingdom game for kids, inspired by Kingshot’s fun loop (build → gather → recruit → quest) without dark themes, PvP, or complex systems.

Dragonhold stays as the deeper D&D strategy project. This is the family-friendly companion.

## Pitch

You are the young ruler of **Little Keep**. Grow your castle, collect food and wood, befriend helpful heroes, and finish gentle quests to earn stars.

## Who it’s for

- Kids roughly **6–12**
- Short sessions (5–15 minutes)
- One device, offline, no chat / no strangers

## Simplified Kingshot loop

| Kingshot idea | Little Keep |
|---------------|-------------|
| Town building | Castle + a few workshops |
| Many resources | Food, Wood, Stars |
| Hero roster | 4 friendly heroes |
| Troop training | Skip — heroes do the quests |
| Research / laws | Skip |
| Hard combat math | Star check + simple power compare |
| Alliances / PvP | None |

## Core loop

1. Collect food & wood from buildings
2. Upgrade the Castle and workshops
3. Recruit hero friends
4. Complete story quests for stars
5. Unlock the next chapter goal

## Heroes (MVP)

1. **Pip the Knight** — brave and sturdy  
2. **Mira the Archer** — quick and clever  
3. **Blink the Mage** — sparkly helper  
4. **Nana the Healer** — kind and caring  

## Quests (gentle)

- Help the baker find flour  
- Shoo mischievous goblins from the garden  
- Rescue a lost kitten  
- Explore the sunny woods  
- Celebrate the Star Fair  

## Visual direction

- Soft sky blue → meadow green → warm gold accents  
- Big tap targets, short sentences, cheerful motion  
- Display: Fredoka · Body: Nunito  
- Avoid dark/grim UI, purple-glow defaults, and dense dashboards  

## Tech

Expo + TypeScript + Expo Router + Zustand + AsyncStorage (same stack as Dragonhold, simpler content).
